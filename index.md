# Homo Garagensis
### Two *humans* making games in a garage.
The *Homo Garagensis* specimen was first documented one rainy afternoon in a garage. It is, at its core, nothing more than a *human*, but one that has developed a remarkable natural adaptation to the heavy machinery, strange gases, deafening sounds, and the inevitable chaos of the garage.

Often found hunched over a keyboard, surrounded by tools and improbable amounts of unidentified compounds, the *Homo Garagensis* spends its days transforming questionable ideas into games.
