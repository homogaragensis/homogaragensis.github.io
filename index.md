The *Homo Garagensis* specimen was first documented one rainy afternoon in a garage. It is, at its core, nothing more than a *human*, but one that has developed a remarkable natural adaptation to the heavy machinery, strange gases, deafening sounds, and the inevitable chaos of the garage.

Often found hunched over a keyboard, surrounded by tools and improbable amounts of unidentified compounds, the *Homo Garagensis* spends its days transforming questionable ideas into games.

## GAL-LINE
This project is a research in both the rediscovery of the simplicity of web technologies, and the fun of programming just for humans. 
The outputs **GAL-LINE** project has two key :

### The Game

We are currently developing our first game: GAL-LINE, a silly arcade game made in JavaScript. 

Make people step back from the yellow line and keep running for as long as you can. 

Collect dozens of crazy characters and put your fancy mechanical keyboard to the test.

### The Book

The simplicity of the game makes it the perfect subject for a tutorial-ish devlog: a book documenting its development from scratch, while also teaching the fundamentals of JavaScript game development.

The book will be published as development progresses, with each section becoming available as soon as it is complete.

[Read it now](https://gal-line-book.github.io)
