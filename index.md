# Homo Garagensis

The *Homo Garagensis* specimen was first documented one rainy afternoon in a garage. It is, at its core, simply a human—one that has developed a remarkable natural adaptation to heavy machinery, strange gases, deafening sounds, and the inevitable chaos of the garage workshop.

Often found hunched over a keyboard, surrounded by tools and improbable amounts of unidentified compounds, *Homo Garagensis* spends its days transforming questionable ideas into playable games.

---

## Featured Project: GAL-LINE

This project is an exploration into both the rediscovery of the simplicity of web technologies and the joy of programming for humans. The **GAL-LINE** project consists of two key parts:

### 1. The Game
A silly arcade game built entirely in JavaScript. 
* **The Goal:** Make people step back from the yellow line and keep running for as long as you can.
* **Features:** Unlock dozens of crazy characters and put your fancy mechanical keyboard to the test.
* **[View Source Code on GitHub](https://github.com/homogaragensis/gal-line)**

### 2. The Devlog Book
The simplicity of the game makes it the perfect subject for a tutorial-style devlog: a book documenting its development from scratch while teaching the fundamentals of JavaScript game development. 

The book is published progressively as development moves forward.

[Read the Book Now](https://homogaragensis.github.io/gal-line-book)
